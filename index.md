<section id="research-projects" class="section">
  <h2 class="section-title"><span class="section-num">04</span>Selected Research Projects</h2>

  <article class="project">
    <header class="project-header">
      <h3>Force-Aware VLA Models for Dexterous Manipulation</h3>
      <p class="project-meta"><strong>Tsinghua&nbsp;SIGS</strong> · Oct&nbsp;2025 – Present · Research Intern</p>
    </header>
    <ul class="project-points">
      <li>Built a bimanual real-robot platform (松灵 7-DoF arms + grippers, Jetson AGX Orin, dual D405 wrist cameras + D435 head camera) collecting joint-position / torque proprioception and depth streams for contact-rich tasks such as optical-module insertion and block placement.</li>
      <li>Reproduced and extended <em>pi0.5</em> and <em>TA-VLA</em>; surveyed diffusion-policy, flow-matching, autoregressive, and hybrid VLA architectures, contrasting their fitness for high-frequency action generation under contact.</li>
      <li><em>Ongoing:</em> investigating <strong>geometric failure modes</strong> of VLA policies and an RGB-D <strong>privileged-geometry distillation</strong> method that uses action-conditioned 2.5D interaction maps to repair contact / placement failures under occlusion and depth ambiguity.</li>
    </ul>
  </article>

  <article class="project">
    <header class="project-header">
      <h3>Reinforcement Learning for Dynamic-Obstacle Local Navigation</h3>
      <p class="project-meta"><strong>Eastern&nbsp;Institute&nbsp;of&nbsp;Technology, Ningbo</strong> · Jan&nbsp;2026 – Present</p>
    </header>
    <ul class="project-points">
      <li>Extending the <em>DRL-DCLP</em> local-planning line of work from static to <strong>dynamic obstacle</strong> settings — formulating the avoidance MDP for robots of varying footprint and designing matching simulation tasks, state space, and rewards.</li>
      <li>Designed an 8-stage curriculum on <em>StageRos</em>: each stage scales the map by ~80%, increases static density, and introduces a hyperparameterised count of dynamic obstacles.</li>
      <li>Implementing a <strong>temporal dynamic encoding</strong> module for multi-obstacle state and fusing it into both policy and value networks; aim is robust generalisation in cluttered, time-varying environments. Targeting <em>T-RO</em> / <em>TMECH</em>.</li>
    </ul>
  </article>

  <article class="project">
    <header class="project-header">
      <h3>RL Locomotion &amp; LiDAR Navigation for Quadrupeds</h3>
      <p class="project-meta"><strong>XJU Innovation Lab</strong> · Jun&nbsp;2025 – Present</p>
    </header>
    <ul class="project-points">
      <li>Trained PPO and N-P3O policies in <em>Isaac Gym</em> / <em>Isaac Lab</em> for multi-terrain quadruped locomotion, generalising across ≥&nbsp;3 terrain types.</li>
      <li>Self-designed quadruped hardware; deployed HIMLoco MuJoCo sim-to-sim and finally sim-to-real, sustaining &gt;&nbsp;10&nbsp;N pushes with stable gait control — walking 0.8 m/s, jogging 2 m/s, crawling — across 4 indoor / outdoor terrain classes and slopes.</li>
      <li>SLAM with <em>fast_lio</em> and <em>point_lio</em>; ROS&nbsp;2 Nav2 stack with A* / Dijkstra global planning and AMCL relocalisation for downstream task scenarios.</li>
      <li><strong>Outcome:</strong> 1 first-author paper accepted at <strong>ICIC&nbsp;2026</strong> (Oral).</li>
    </ul>
  </article>

  <article class="project">
    <header class="project-header">
      <h3>Deep-Learning Strawberry Disease &amp; Pest Monitoring</h3>
      <p class="project-meta"><strong>Xinjiang University</strong> · Jun&nbsp;2024 – Jan&nbsp;2026</p>
    </header>
    <ul class="project-points">
      <li>YOLOv8 detection pipeline with attention modules and an improved feature-fusion neck — <strong>+15&nbsp;%</strong> recall on small targets and <strong>+25&nbsp;%</strong> precision; pruned and quantised for embedded deployment.</li>
      <li>Inverse-kinematics solver for an arm mounted on a tracked rover; coordinated chassis-arm motion to acquire clear, comprehensive plant imagery from preset observation viewpoints.</li>
      <li><strong>Outcome:</strong> National Innovation &amp; Entrepreneurship grant — <em>Outstanding</em> close-out · 1 design patent (1st author) · 1 software copyright (3rd author).</li>
    </ul>
  </article>
